## Why v02 beats v01

v01 missed the day's two biggest breaking stories. v02 puts them on the front page as full panels:

- **Iran strikes US bases in Bahrain** — Tehran says it hit two US positions (al-Riffa and Awali); Bahrain reports an industrial fire, no injuries. (Al Jazeera / Euronews)
- **Mexico captures FBI most-wanted cartel boss "Chapo Isidro"** — special forces seized Fausto Isidro Meza Flores in Guasave, Sinaloa; gunfights and blockades followed, at least 11 dead. (BBC / CBS)

The ticker adds three more stories v01 lacked entirely: the Russian strike on Kryvyi Rih, the AI-assisted Riemann proof claim, and the cargo ship ablaze in the Gulf of Finland. All facts verified from 21 Nov 2026 reporting (oneturn.news day page, Al Jazeera, BBC, CBS, PBS NewsHour).

## The image

Six panels + a ticker: Bahrain, Mexico, Kyiv air assault, AI sell-off, ICJ Myanmar genocide ruling, COP31 overtime. Ticker: Poland missiles / Kryvyi Rih, Gulf of Finland cargo ship, Riemann proof, Peru quake, Trump–Putin ties, Gaza food-truck strike.

`main.py` copies `entry.png` to the output directory and validates it (PNG signature, 1024×1024, ≤1 MiB, post-copy byte-equality check), so both runs write the same bytes. No network.
